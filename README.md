## Samuel David Egwu

**Lead AI/ML Engineer — production agentic AI, LLM evaluation, and the safety rails that let a model operate near real money.**

I build AI systems that have to survive contact with real users, real data and real money.

Currently at [BrandDrive](https://branddrive.co/), where I lead a production multi-agent platform sitting in front of a live banking product — over 10 specialist agents over 280 schema-validated tools, at roughly $0.007 per request. I also built the evaluation system underneath it, which moved quality from 55% to consistently above 95%.

### What I work on

- **Agentic systems in production** — coordinator/specialist topologies, scoped tool catalogues, and keeping context small while the platform's surface keeps growing.
- **LLM evaluation** — versioned golden datasets, multi-turn conversation tests, version-pinned judge rubrics, and quality gates that block a bad release rather than discovering it in production.
- **Safety boundaries** — the model proposes, a validator decides, an executor acts. Money paths stay deterministic, so a bad generation is a bad sentence rather than a bad transaction.
- **Cost and latency** — task-aware model routing, prompt caching, streaming, and optimising the whole path rather than just the model choice.
